How to find us

Heidelberg main station (Hauptbahnhof) is a 12–15 minute tram ride from the Neuenheim campus. Lines 21, 24, and bus 32 all reach Bunsengymnasium or Jahnstraße, two minutes’ walk from the building. From the airport, take a regional train to Heidelberg Hbf via Mannheim.

The COS occupies the building Im Neuenheimer Feld 230. The Lohmann lab is on the 1st floor.

For accessible-route guidance and floor plans, see the official COS ‘Maps & directions’ page.
